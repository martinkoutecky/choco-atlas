But to preserve the network structure, a type must capture counterpart exposures as well as endowments and liabilities. Compressing many institutions into a few types therefore needs extra symmetry or a type-to-type liability model. That could make a sensible continuous financial-network project, but it would be a new computational study of clearing—not a mirror of any named result in this paper. The paper’s uniqueness and impossibility theorems provide no computational claim for that study to continue.
