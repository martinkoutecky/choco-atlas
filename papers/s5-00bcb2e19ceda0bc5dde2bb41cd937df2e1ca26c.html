The case FOR (proponent)

The strongest positive lead is Contribution 5: the sampling and inference framework for majority outcomes. In a mass-electorate setting, let each type \(t\) be a permissible preference relation and let \(\mu_t\) be its population share. Given incomplete survey reports and a specified observation model, one could ask which population majority relations remain compatible with the data. A solution would give the attainable relations, with a witnessing distribution \(\mu\) for each one. This is a sensible high-multiplicity regime: millions of respondents may occupy a much smaller set of preference types.
