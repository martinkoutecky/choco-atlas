The case against its *programme value* is narrower. A more realistic model might constrain outages to shared regions, network components, or service capacities. Then the feasibility and cost model for those infrastructure actions supplies much of the combinatorial difficulty; the population distribution mainly supplies the election scores. That could still be a useful study, but it would be a newly constructed infrastructure-and-election problem prompted by the paper’s concern.
