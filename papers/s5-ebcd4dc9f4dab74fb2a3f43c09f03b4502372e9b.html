The best candidate mirror would put masses of preference, awareness, and experience types on each side of a large matching market, represent matches as flows between types, and ask for an absorbing stable flow or the convergence of a type-level rematching process. A nontrivial limit therefore needs a new rule for moving positive mass, or a rate-based interaction model. Supplying the missing rules would create a new dynamic model, not preserve the computational task of either proposition.
