That is a coherent research question, but making it computational requires choices the paper does not make: how to encode utilities and rules, how to represent allocations across types, and what counts as a deviation in a nonatomic society. In that setting, an individual has measure zero and cannot change aggregate production. Treating each positive-mass type as a strategic bloc restores influence only by changing the players from individuals to coordinated groups. Either model could support a new implementation-theory project, but neither mirrors a computational result in this paper.
