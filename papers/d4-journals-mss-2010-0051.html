A high-multiplicity version could describe a continuum of people by types encoding their complete linked preferences, scale the cakes per capita, and ask for an envy-free allocation of mass among bundles. That is the strongest plausible extension. But it adds type distributions, mass assignments, and resource constraints that the paper’s theorems do not address. It may be a sensible fair-division problem; it does not give a computational mirror of Theorems 3, 6, 7, or 8.
