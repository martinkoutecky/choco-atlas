That upstream process offers the best candidate for a broader mirror, but it exposes a real modelling difficulty. Both strategies combine evidence across sources, and their operations depend on source multiplicity. With a fixed per-person report, a unit-mass population does not preserve those effects as a well-behaved function of type proportions. One could introduce weighted or infinitesimal evidence to retain a non-degenerate limit, but that changes the fusion operator and poses a new computational question rather than mirroring one of this paper’s results.

That is a strong reason to reject the proposed anchors, but it does not establish the universal claim that no scenario could yield a worthwhile mirror. The honest negative case is therefore narrow: this paper supplies no qualifying computational result to anchor a continuous mirror, and its most plausible extension needs a new model.
