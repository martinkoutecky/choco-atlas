The case AGAINST (opponent, writing after the proponent)

The proponent raises no eligible anchors, and that is the strongest case against a continuous mirror here. The paper’s results concern the semantics of dynamic preferences, a constructive characterization, and preservation of properties of Lehmann’s System C. The best candidate for a mirror would be a new task such as computing preferred assumption sets or testing whether a conclusion follows. If they differ, computing an aggregate answer requires a population-level aggregation semantics that this paper does not define. Either way, the proposed task would be new computational work built on the paper’s semantics, not a continuous version of a result the paper establishes.
