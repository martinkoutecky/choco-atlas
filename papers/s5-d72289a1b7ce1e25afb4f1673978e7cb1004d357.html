The strongest proposed mirror is fair redesign, but its mass assignment must be constrained by the paper’s actual allocation rule. Designers choose building edits \(\delta\); those edits determine residents’ entry probabilities, and iterative proportional fitting then determines their location probabilities. The system does not choose an arbitrary assignment \(x\) to minimize inequality. Leaving \(x\) free would let the optimizer redistribute residents in ways the paper’s behavioral model forbids. A more faithful version would define a population measure \(\mu\) over complete resident types, including baseline utility, and minimize \(\epsilon_2\) of the benefits induced by the continuum IPF allocation \(P_\delta(\mu)\), subject to the design constraints. That is a plausible continuous question, and it appears close to the authors’ recommendation task.
