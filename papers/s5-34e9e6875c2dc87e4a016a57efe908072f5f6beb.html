Proposition 9 is the closest-looking candidate: it shows that, in the paper’s climate and savings examples, stronger disagreement aversion raises the optimal action under distribution-averaging, while stronger ambiguity aversion lowers it under expected-utility-averaging. But that is a comparative-statics result, not a computational one, so it cannot serve as the required anchor.

A continuum of expert-belief types might be a plausible extension of the model. The paper, however, studies a finite list of experts and does not establish a computational result whose continuous mirror could be stated here. So there is no honest per-anchor continuous problem to hand to a prover.

The case AGAINST (opponent, writing after the proponent)

The proponent identifies no surviving computational anchor, and the paper supports that reading. Lemma 1 and Propositions 1–6 characterize preference properties and representations; Propositions 7–9 give comparative statics. None states a complexity classification or gives an algorithm for a computational problem. The paper has one decision-maker consulting \(N\) experts, not a society of agents whose computational social-choice problem is being solved.

Proposition 9 is the closest candidate. Its continuous-expert version could model a large forecasting community by a measure over belief types and ask how to choose mitigation or savings. That is plausible as a new research direction. It does not mirror Proposition 9’s result, which compares optimal actions as disagreement aversion changes. The paper supplies no complexity problem behind that comparison. A computational version would also need a finite input representation for the continuum and a rule for aggregating its belief distribution: the paper’s averager is defined on \([0,1]^N\), with no consistency condition across different \(N\). Choosing those ingredients would define a new model and problem.

Continuity already appears in the paper’s outcome interval, prospects, and action choices. Making experts continuous would change the expertise object; it would not continuize a population in the programme’s sense. So the strongest defensible negative verdict is that this paper has no worthwhile, paper-anchored continuous mirror.
