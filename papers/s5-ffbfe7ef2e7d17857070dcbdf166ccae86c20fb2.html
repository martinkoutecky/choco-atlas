The strongest positive setting is centralized college placement, the paper’s own example: millions of students compete for seats, while many may share the same preferences and schools’ priorities. A type would be a complete preference ordering (for students) or priority ordering (for schools); mass would be each type’s fraction of the market. The assignment variable would be \(x_{pq}\), the mass of student type \(p\) assigned to school type \(q\), with school capacities scaled to the population. Fractional type-level assignments are consistent with each individual student receiving an integral seat.
