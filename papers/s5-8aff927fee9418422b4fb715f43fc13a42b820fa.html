The resulting question would study an aggregate dispatch market, with a scaled task stream and evolving schedules. If types also include those schedules, the support may grow with the fleet; if they omit them, the model risks losing the feasibility and switching-time details the paper’s bidding algorithm uses. This is a possible new research direction, but it is not a continuous formulation of a named result in this paper.
