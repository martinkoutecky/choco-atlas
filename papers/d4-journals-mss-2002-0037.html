The paper defines a formula for the rule, but gives no input representation or running-time claim from which to anchor a complexity mirror.

A plausible population setting would be a large municipality sharing infrastructure costs: types are resident cohorts with the same multicommodity demands, stand-alone costs, and demand-scaling paths; \(\mu_t\) is each cohort’s population share. One could ask for the per-capita charges under a mass version of the Path Serial Rule, with each incremental cost divided among the mass of cohorts still above the relevant stand-alone-cost threshold. This is a plausible extension of the paper’s cost-sharing problem, and its natural-gas example gives the authors a reason to recognize the setting.
