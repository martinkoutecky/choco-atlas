The strongest replacement mirror is nevertheless clear. Let each type be a complete individual preference tournament, with mass \(\mu_t\), and seek a ranking \(\pi\) minimizing
\[ \sum_t \mu_t\,d_H(t,\pi). \]
This preserves the rule’s stated objective and has a plausible high-multiplicity interpretation: a large population of evaluators can share the same pairwise judgments. The mass objective does not lose information the rule uses, and it does not degenerate. It is weighted feedback arc set on the aggregate pairwise weights. Weighted feedback arc set has a PTAS, and high-multiplicity Kemeny winner determination has been studied; under the programme’s own standard, that high-multiplicity work supports the regime rather than disqualifying it as prior art. [Kenyon-Mathieu and Schudy on weighted feedback arc set](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/eccc-reports/2006/TR06-144/index.html), [Fitzsimmons and Hemaspaandra on high-multiplicity elections](https://mathcs.holycross.edu/~zfitzsim/papers/Fitzsimmons-Hemaspaandra2019_Article_High-multiplicityElectionProbl.pdf).
