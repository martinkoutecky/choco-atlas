Its unnumbered claim in §4.4 that the ILPs are NP-hard does not supply such a result or a reduction.

That claim also appears unsupported by the models as written. With fixed course capacities, the constraints \(\sum_j x_{ij}=v\) and \(\sum_i x_{ij}=q_j\), together with \(x_{ij}\in\{0,1\}\), describe capacitated bipartite matching with linear edge weights. That is a min-cost flow problem. The lexicographic objectives do not by themselves establish hardness. So the paper offers no sound complexity result for the proposed mirrors to inherit.
