The nearest plausible mirror would be a new apportionment problem for a federation with many similar jurisdictions. A type \(t\) records a jurisdiction’s population \(p_t\) and current seat allotment \(b_t\); \(\mu_t\) is the fraction of jurisdictions of that type. Given rational type masses, integer seat bounds \(m,M\), and an average seat budget \(h\), choose an integer allotment \(s_t\in[m,M]\) for each type to minimize \(\sum_t\mu_t|s_t-b_t|\), subject to \(\sum_t\mu_ts_t=h\) and, whenever \(p_t<p_u\), \(s_t\le s_u\) and \(p_t/s_t\le p_u/s_u\).
