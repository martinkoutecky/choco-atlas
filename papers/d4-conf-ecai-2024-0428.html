The most tempting alternative is to continuize the buyer population while preserving network roles. Yet these mechanisms use individual neighbor sets, reachability, and priority to create incentives. Treating those as type-level averages risks losing the individual referral leverage that their incentive arguments depend on. Treating the exact network position and neighbors as part of each type preserves that information, but can leave nearly every buyer in a distinct type. A role-based or cohort network could be a sensible new model, though it would need its own incentive notion and results.

So the proponent raises no viable continuous-mirror anchor, and the paper itself offers little basis for one. That is a strong case against spending this programme’s effort on this paper.
