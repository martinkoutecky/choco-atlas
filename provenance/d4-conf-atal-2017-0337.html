The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given a finite role graph \(H=D\star c\), positive role masses \(\mu\in\Delta(T)\), nonatomic valuation oracles \(v_t\), and a scaled cake supply \(\lambda\) per unit population, form the complete bipartite blow-up in which every role-\t\ agent compares with every role-\u\ agent whenever \t\to u\. Output a measurable allocation law \(\nu_t\) over individual bundles, realizable by disjoint bundles in the scaled cake, such that for every edge \t\to u\, every \(B\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_t\), and every \(B'\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_u\), \(v_t(B)\ge v_t(B')\); determine whether this can be computed by a type-level Robertson–Webb protocol using queries polynomial in \(|T|\) and independent of the replication factor.

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

A society is given by its support: a list of \(\tau\) types \(T\), each with positive binary-rational mass \(\mu_t\), with \(\sum_t\mu_t=1\). Here \(\tau\) is the support size, not the number of possible types.
Let \(H=D\star c\), where \(D\) is a nonempty DAG on \(T\setminus\{c\}\); retain its directed edges and add both \(t\to c\) and \(c\to t\) for every other type. There are no comparisons within a type.
Represent the population by \([0,1]\), partitioned into intervals \(P_t\) of lengths \(\mu_t\). The cake is \(C=[0,\lambda]\), where \(\lambda>0\) is a binary rational. Each type has a nonnegative, countably additive, nonatomic Borel valuation \(v_t\), normalized by \(v_t(C)=1\), accessible through Robertson–Webb queries.
An allocation is a measurable assignment \(p\mapsto B_p\) whose bundles partition all of \(C\) into pairwise disjoint finite unions of intervals; empty bundles and degenerate intervals are allowed. Its finite representation consists of arithmetic and comparison formulas for bundle endpoints, using input constants and oracle replies.
For each type, \(\nu_t\) is the bundle law induced by normalized Lebesgue measure on \(P_t\). Take support in the bundle space with pseudometric \(d(B,B')=\operatorname{Leb}(B\triangle B')+\sum_{s\in T}v_s(B\triangle B')\), identifying distance-zero bundles.
Require \(v_t(B)\geq v_t(B')\) for every edge \(t\to u\) and every \(B\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_t\), \(B'\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_u\). A protocol may query each type oracle directly and need not use a single cutter.
Is computing such an allocation polynomial-time solvable in the type-level Robertson–Webb model, with time and query bounds polynomial in \(m,\tau,L\) and independent of any finite replication factor, or is it NP-hard already for explicitly encoded rational piecewise-constant valuations?
Here \(m=1\) is the number of cake components and \(L\) is the finite input bit length, including valuation descriptions when supplied; exact oracle replies and arithmetic on them have unit cost.
For finitely many agents on a cone of a DAG, a locally envy-free complete allocation can be computed using \(O(n^2)\) Robertson–Webb queries. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/1611.06589)

Modeling choices made when packaging the task

  • The distribution over roles was realized as a continuum population of total mass one, partitioned into type intervals of the specified masses.
  • Scaled cake supply was interpreted literally as one interval of length lambda per unit total population; individual bundles must form an actual disjoint partition, rather than satisfy an aggregate fractional capacity constraint.
  • Valuations were made nonnegative, countably additive nonatomic Borel measures normalized to value the entire scaled cake at one.
  • The role graph uses a nonempty DAG and bidirectional apex edges; the blow-up adds no comparisons within a role.
  • Complete allocation was restored. Empty bundles and degenerate intervals remain admissible, consistently with the discrete bundle domain.
  • The undefined topology for bundle-law support was specified using symmetric-difference measure for Lebesgue measure and all type valuations.
  • A finite symbolic representation of the continuum assignment was specified; its type laws are induced by that assignment.
  • Polynomial computation was distinguished from query count by charging for arithmetic operations as well as queries. NP-hardness was given a finite encoding through rational piecewise-constant valuations.
  • The otherwise absent parameter m was set to one cake component, and L was defined as the finite input bit length.

Prover output (gpt-6.1-sol / high, Bolzano prover prompt)

1. Classification

The problem as explicitly defined is polynomial-time solvable, with zero Robertson–Webb queries.

The immediate witness is \(B_p=\{\lambda p\}\). More substantially, the degeneracy is unavoidable: every admissible measurable allocation has the same bundle law, concentrated at the empty-bundle class, for every type. Thus there is no case with positive-value bundles for a positive-mass population under the stated requirements.

2. Structural lemma: only countably many people can receive positive-value bundles

Define the finite Borel measure
\( q=\operatorname{Leb}+\sum_{s\in T}v_s \)
on \(C\). Its total mass is
\( q(C)=\lambda+\tau. \)
The given pseudometric is exactly
\( d(B,B')=q(B\triangle B'). \)

Consider any admissible allocation \((B_p)_{p\in[0,1]}\). For each positive integer \(k\), put
\( E_k=\{p:q(B_p)\geq 1/k\}. \)

Claim. \(E_k\) is finite.

Indeed, if \(p_1,\ldots,p_N\) are distinct members of \(E_k\), pairwise disjointness and finite additivity give
\( N/k\leq\sum_{i=1}^{N}q(B_{p_i}) =q\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}B_{p_i}\right) \leq\lambda+\tau. \)
An infinite \(E_k\) would contain finite subsets of arbitrarily large cardinality, contradicting this bound.

Every strictly positive real number is at least \(1/k\) for some positive integer \(k\). Consequently,
\( E:=\{p:q(B_p)>0\}=\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty}E_k \)
is countable.

For every \(p\notin E\),
\( d(B_p,\varnothing)=q(B_p)=0. \)
Thus all but countably many people receive the empty-bundle class in the specified quotient space. This argument uses only measurability of individual bundles, pairwise disjointness, and finiteness of \(q\); restricting bundles to finite unions of intervals does not avert it.

3. Consequence: every support comparison is automatic

Write \(o=[\varnothing]\) for the empty-bundle class. Each type interval \(P_t\) has positive Lebesgue measure \(\mu_t\). Since \(E\) is countable,
\( \frac{\operatorname{Leb}(E\cap P_t)}{\mu_t}=0. \)
Therefore the induced law is
\( \nu_t=\delta_o \)
for every type \(t\).

Its support is exactly \(\{o\}\): every neighborhood of \(o\) has probability one, while any other point has an open ball excluding \(o\), hence of probability zero.

Valuations are well-defined on equivalence classes. For any bundles \(B,B'\),
\( |v_t(B)-v_t(B')| \leq v_t(B\triangle B') \leq d(B,B'). \)
In particular, every representative of \(o\) has value zero under every type valuation.

It follows that, for every edge \(t\to u\) and every pair of supported bundles, the required inequality is simply
\( 0\geq 0. \)
Hence every admissible measurable allocation satisfies the fairness requirement, regardless of the comparison graph.

4. Explicit complete allocation and complexity

Output the endpoint formula
\( B_p=[\lambda p,\lambda p], \qquad p\in[0,1]. \)

This satisfies every requirement:

  • Each bundle is an allowed degenerate interval.
  • Since \(\lambda>0\), distinct people receive distinct singletons, so bundles are pairwise disjoint.
  • For every \(x\in C\), the person \(p=x/\lambda\) receives \(x\). Thus their union is exactly \(C\).
  • Nonatomicity gives \(v_s(\{\lambda p\})=0\) for every \(s,p\), and singleton Lebesgue measure is zero. The assignment into the quotient bundle space is therefore constant and measurable.
  • Both endpoints use one arithmetic expression involving the population parameter \(p\) and the input constant \(\lambda\).

The protocol makes zero oracle queries. Its output has size \(O(L)\), allowing for the binary encoding of \(\lambda\), and can be produced in polynomial time. These bounds are independent of any replication factor.

The same construction applies to explicitly encoded rational piecewise-constant valuations.

5. Why the substantive continuum extension fails here

The structural lemma addresses more than the singleton witness. For every admissible allocation and every type \(t\),
\( \operatorname{Leb}\{p\in P_t:v_t(B_p)>0\}=0. \)
Indeed, this set is contained in the countable set \(E\). Thus requiring positive own-value bundles on any population subset of positive mass would make the problem infeasible.

Completeness does not prevent this: countable additivity does not constrain an uncountable union of singleton bundles.

The cited finite-agent protocol uses finitely many equally valued pieces followed by choices in topological order. [Abebe, Kleinberg, and Parkes, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1611.06589). The continuum formulation here instead forces all type-level bundle laws to collapse to \(\delta_o\). A substantive extension would require changing the allocation or resource-accounting model; it cannot be obtained while retaining all the present requirements.
