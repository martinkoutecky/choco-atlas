The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given a finite type set \(T\), \(\mu\in\Delta(T)\), and explicitly represented piecewise-constant nonatomic valuations \(V_t\) repeated on a per-capita replicated interval \(K_N\), compute a finite family of bundle templates and a feasible mass flow \(x_{t,P}\ge0\), with \(\sum_P x_{t,P}=\mu_t\), resource-capacity constraints, and an integral realization whenever \(N\mu_t\) and \(Nx_{t,P}\) are integral, such that all of \(K_N\) is allocated and, whenever \(x_{t,P}>0\), \(V_t(P)\ge V_t(Q)\) for every used template \(Q\).

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

Definitions

Let \(C=[0,1]\), let \(T\) be finite, and let \(\mu\in\Delta(T)\) have rational entries. Each type \(t\) has a nonnegative piecewise-constant valuation \(V_t\), specified by rational breakpoints and rational densities. A template is a finite union \(P\subseteq C\) of rational-endpoint intervals.

A type-mass allocation is a finite template family \(\mathcal P\) and rational \(x_{t,P}\geq0\) with \(\sum_{P\in\mathcal P}x_{t,P}=\mu_t\) and \(\sum_{t,P:z\in P}x_{t,P}=1\) for almost every \(z\in C\). For each \(N\) with \(N\mu_t,Nx_{t,P}\in\mathbb Z\), it must be realizable on \(K_N=C\times[N]\): allocate all of \(K_N\) to \(N\mu_t\) agents of type \(t\), with \(Nx_{t,P}\) such agents receiving a replicated copy of \(P\). It is envy-free if \(x_{t,P}>0\) implies \(V_t(P)\geq V_t(Q)\) for every used \(Q\).

Target

Is computing such a complete envy-free type-mass allocation in \(\mathsf{P}\), or is it \(\mathsf{NP}\)-hard?

Nearest known results

For finitely many individually specified agents with arbitrary nonatomic valuations, a bounded Robertson--Webb protocol computes a complete envy-free allocation. For explicitly represented piecewise-constant valuations, polynomial-time algorithms are known for ordinary finite-agent envy-free cake cutting. Neither result treats the type-mass template-and-realisability problem above.
