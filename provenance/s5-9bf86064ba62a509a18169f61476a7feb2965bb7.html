The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given an integer \(\rho\ge5\), a finite type set \(T\) with distribution \(\mu\), chore classes \(E\) with \(\rho q_e\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\), and additive costs \(c_\theta\), minimize \(\alpha\) over nonnegative flows \(x^0_{\theta,b},x^1_{\theta,b}\) on integral bundles \(b\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^{E}\), satisfying \(\sum_b(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b})=\rho\mu_\theta\), \(\sum_{\theta,b}b_e(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b})=\rho q_e\), and \(\sum_{\theta,b}x^1_{\theta,b}\le1\), where the nonexceptional support has \(c_\theta(b)\le\lambda_{\theta,\rho}(q)\), the exceptional support has \(c_\theta(b)\le\alpha\lambda_{\theta,\rho}(q)\), and \(\lambda_{\theta,\rho}(q)=\min_{\sum_{j=1}^{\rho}b^j=\rho q}\max_j c_\theta(b^j)\).

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

A society is given by its support: a list \(T\) of \(\tau\) types with positive binary-rational masses \(\mu_\theta\), summing to \(1\); \(\tau\) counts supported types, not possible types.
Given an integer \(\rho\ge5\), chore classes \(E\), binary-rational supplies \(q_e\ge0\) with \(K_e=\rho q_e\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\), and binary-rational costs \(c_{\theta,e}\ge0\), let \(m=\sum_e K_e\) and \(c_\theta(b)=\sum_e c_{\theta,e}b_e\). Measure algorithmic running times in \(m,\tau,L,\rho,|E|\), where \(L\) is the total input bit length.
Allowed bundles are \(B=\{b\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^{E}:b_e\le K_e\}\), including the empty bundle. Define \(\lambda_\theta=\min_{b^1,\ldots,b^\rho\in B:\,\sum_j b^j=K}\max_j c_\theta(b^j)\); these benchmarks are defined by the input, not supplied separately.
For \(\alpha\ge1\), feasible flows \(x^0_{\theta,b},x^1_{\theta,b}\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}\) satisfy \(\sum_b(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b})=\rho\mu_\theta\), \(\sum_{\theta,b}b_e(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b})=K_e\) for every \(e\), and \(\sum_{\theta,b}x^1_{\theta,b}\le1\).
Positive \(x^0_{\theta,b}\) requires \(c_\theta(b)\le\lambda_\theta\); positive \(x^1_{\theta,b}\) requires \(c_\theta(b)\le\alpha\lambda_\theta\). Exceptional mass may be distributed across types, and inequalities include equality; zero benchmarks retain these literal constraints.
Does every such society admit feasible flows with \(\alpha\le\frac{(\rho+1)^2}{4\rho}\), or are there counterexamples to this approximation guarantee?
For discrete additive chores and \(n\ge5\) agents, allocations exist giving all but one agent cost at most her MMS and the remaining agent cost at most \(\frac{(n+1)^2}{4n}\) times her MMS. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.12347v1).
No result settling this precise distribution-over-types flow guarantee was located in the literature search.

Modeling choices made when packaging the task

  • Normalized the distribution to total mass one, so scaled society mass is rho and the exceptional budget is one agent-equivalent.
  • Specified nonnegative binary-rational supplies and additive per-chore costs, with total input bit length L.
  • Restricted each integral bundle to the available inventory, as a discrete bundle is a subset of the chores; empty bundles remain allowed.
  • Required alpha to be at least one, following the approximation convention.
  • Retained zero costs and zero MMS benchmarks without normalization or division.
  • Defined m as the total number of chores in the scaled inventory and treated MMS benchmarks as derived quantities rather than additional input.

Result

Every society in the question admits feasible flows with $\alpha=1$, which is stronger than the proposed guarantee.

This holds for the full stated model, with arbitrary supported type masses. The key is that the resource constraints concern aggregate flows: different types can use different MMS partitions.

Construction and proof

For each type $\theta$, choose a partition attaining its benchmark:
$$ b^{\theta,1},\ldots,b^{\theta,\rho}\in B, \qquad \sum_{j=1}^{\rho}b^{\theta,j}=K, \qquad c_\theta(b^{\theta,j})\le\lambda_\theta \quad\text{for every }j. $$
Such a partition exists. Indeed, $B$ is finite, and the set of admissible partitions is nonempty because it contains $(K,0,\ldots,0)$. Thus the minimum defining $\lambda_\theta$ is attained.

For $\theta\in T$ and $b\in B$, define the multiplicity
$$ r_{\theta,b} = \bigl|\{j\in\{1,\ldots,\rho\}:b^{\theta,j}=b\}\bigr|. $$
Set
$$ x^0_{\theta,b}=\mu_\theta r_{\theta,b}, \qquad x^1_{\theta,b}=0. $$
Multiplicities ensure that repeated bundles, including repeated empty bundles, are counted correctly.

We verify every constraint.

Type masses. Since each chosen partition has exactly $\rho$ bundles,
$$ \sum_{b\in B}(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b}) = \mu_\theta\sum_{b\in B}r_{\theta,b} = \rho\mu_\theta. $$

Chore supplies. For each $e\in E$,
$$ \begin{aligned} \sum_{\theta\in T}\sum_{b\in B} b_e(x^0_{\theta,b}+x^1_{\theta,b}) &= \sum_{\theta\in T}\mu_\theta \sum_{j=1}^{\rho}b^{\theta,j}_e\\ &= \sum_{\theta\in T}\mu_\theta K_e\\ &=K_e, \end{aligned} $$
where the last equality uses $\sum_{\theta\in T}\mu_\theta=1$.

Cost restrictions. If $x^0_{\theta,b}>0$, then $b$ occurs in the chosen MMS partition for $\theta$, so
$$ c_\theta(b)\le\lambda_\theta. $$
This reasoning also applies when $\lambda_\theta=0$: every used bundle then has cost zero. No division by a benchmark is required.

Exceptional mass. By construction,
$$ \sum_{\theta,b}x^1_{\theta,b}=0\le1. $$
Consequently, these flows are feasible with $\alpha=1$.

Finally,
$$ \frac{(\rho+1)^2}{4\rho}-1 = \frac{(\rho-1)^2}{4\rho}\ge0. $$
Hence they satisfy the requested guarantee for every $\rho\ge5$. In fact, the construction works for every positive integer $\rho$.

Constructivity and the discrete comparison

The benchmarks need not be supplied separately. One exact construction expands the supplies into $m$ labeled chore copies and, for each type, enumerates their $\rho^m$ assignments to $\rho$ labeled bundles. Every admissible bundle partition is represented. Computing its maximum cost and retaining a minimizer therefore determines $\lambda_\theta$ and a witnessing partition using exact rational arithmetic.

This gives a running-time bound of
$$ \tau\rho^m\operatorname{poly}(m,\rho,|E|,L), $$
and the resulting flow has at most $\tau\rho$ nonzero entries. This is an existence proof and an exact finite algorithm; it does not assert polynomial-time computability.

The discrete theorem is unnecessary here. In the stated flow model, each type receives its own MMS partition with each partition position weighted by $\mu_\theta$. Each type thereby contributes exactly $\mu_\theta K$ to aggregate supply, making the partitions compatible through the flow equations.
