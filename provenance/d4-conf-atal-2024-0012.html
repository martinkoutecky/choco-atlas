The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given a rational finite-support society μ of unit-size transaction types and capacity shares \(0<κ<ρ<1\), let reports form a mass distribution ν; include the top ρ mass and confirm the top κ mass. Optimize over anonymous linear rebate rules \(R_C^K(ν)=a+\int Q^C_ν(q)\,dK(q)\) and \(R_U^K(ν)=a+\int Q^U_ν(q)\,dK(q)\), where \(K\) is a finite signed measure over included ranks and the quantiles use the leave-one-out, one-sided rank limits for confirmed and unconfirmed units. For every ν, require restricted truthfulness and user individual rationality, require \(κ R_C^K+(ρ-κ)R_U^K\le κ p_{\mathrm{in}}\), and maximize \(f\) subject to \(κ R_C^K\ge fκ p_{\mathrm{out}}\). Here \(p_{\mathrm{in}}\) is the lowest confirmed-side cutoff bid and \(p_{\mathrm{out}}\) the highest unconfirmed-side cutoff bid, allowing a gap between them. Return the optimal rule and its allocation and payments on μ.
