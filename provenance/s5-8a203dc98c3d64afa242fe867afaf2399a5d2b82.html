The continuous mirror question, as graded

Choose action masses \(z_t(s,a)\ge 0\) with \(\sum_a z_t(s,a)=x_t(s)\), \(\sum_{s,a}d_k(a)z_t(s,a)\le\beta_k\), and \(x_{t+1}(s')=\sum_{s,a}z_t(s,a)p(s'\mid s,a)\). Maximize the continuum GGF of individuals’ ex-ante discounted values, \(\rho_W(V)=\int_0^1 W(q)V^{\uparrow}(q)\,dq\), for a normalized nonincreasing rank-weight density \(W\). Under anonymous allocation, individual ex-ante values are equal, so this objective equals mean discounted reward. For fixed \(\gamma\), find a feasible policy within additive \(\varepsilon\) of optimal.
