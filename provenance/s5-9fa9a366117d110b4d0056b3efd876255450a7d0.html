The continuous mirror question, as graded

For a fixed query \(x\) and finite reply set \(\mathcal{Y}\), given \(q\) advertiser types with rational masses \(\mu_\theta\), reward functions \(r_\theta\), reference distribution \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\), proposal distribution \(\pi_{\mathrm{gen}}\), and per-capita weight \(\lambda>0\), approximate in total variation the policy maximizing \(\mathbb{E}_{y\sim\pi}[\sum_\theta\mu_\theta r_\theta(x,y)]-\lambda D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\Vert\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})\). Using \(M\) proposals and weights proportional to \(\frac{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y_j\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{gen}}(y_j\mid x)}\exp(\sum_\theta\mu_\theta r_\theta(x,y_j)/\lambda)\), determine the sample count and running time needed for error at most \(\varepsilon\) with probability at least \(1-\delta\), in terms of \(q\), input size, and proposal-overlap parameters. For a finite population of \(n\) advertisers, this recovers the per-capita objective with \(\lambda=\tau/n\).
