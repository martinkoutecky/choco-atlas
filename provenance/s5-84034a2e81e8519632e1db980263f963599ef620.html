The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given rational location types \(z_t\in\mathbb{Q}^2\) with rational masses \(\mu_t\geq 0\) summing to \(1\), a recommended site \(p\in\mathbb{Q}^2\), and rational \(\lambda\in[0,1)\), does the coordinatewise weighted median of the resident distribution together with advice mass \(\lambda\delta_p\) remain strategyproof on every common-denominator finite refinement and satisfy \(C_\mu(F_\lambda(\mu,p))/\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)\leq\min\{\sqrt{2}\rho,\rho+\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2\lambda^2+2}/(1-\lambda)\}\), where \(C_\mu(a)=\sum_t\mu_t\|z_t-a\|_2\), \(\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)=\min_a C_\mu(a)>0\), and \(\rho=C_\mu(p)/\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)\)?

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

A society is specified by its support: distinct locations \(z_t\in\mathbb{Q}^2\), with positive binary rational masses \(\mu_t\) satisfying \(\sum_{t=1}^{\tau}\mu_t=1\). Here \(\tau\) is the support size, not the number of possible types. Let \(p\in\mathbb{Q}^2\) and \(\lambda\in\mathbb{Q}\cap[0,1)\) be fixed independently of reports. State any running times in the dimension \(m=2\), support size \(\tau\), and total input bit length \(L\).
For any reported distribution \(\nu\) of total mass one, define \(F_\lambda(\nu,p)\) by taking each coordinate's lower weighted median under \(\nu+\lambda\delta_p\): the smallest coordinate whose cumulative mass is at least \((1+\lambda)/2\).
Facilities and individual reports may lie anywhere in \(\mathbb{R}^2\); an individual's cost is Euclidean distance from their true location, and there are no payments.
A common-denominator finite refinement chooses any positive integer \(N\) with \(N\mu_t\) and \(N\lambda\) integral, replaces type \(t\) by \(N\mu_t\) individuals of mass \(1/N\), and applies the same rule to their reports. Strategyproofness concerns unilateral individual deviations, with all other reports, masses, \(p\), and \(\lambda\) fixed.
Define \(C_\mu(a)=\sum_t\mu_t\lVert z_t-a\rVert_2\), \(\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)=\min_{a\in\mathbb{R}^2}C_\mu(a)>0\), and \(\rho=C_\mu(p)/\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)\).

Does \(F_\lambda\) remain strategyproof on every common-denominator finite refinement and, for every admissible input, satisfy \(\frac{C_\mu(F_\lambda(\mu,p))}{\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)}\leq\min\{\sqrt{2}\rho,\rho+\sqrt{2},\frac{\sqrt{2\lambda^2+2}}{1-\lambda}\}\)?

For finite unweighted societies, adding \(\lfloor\lambda n\rfloor\) advice copies and taking coordinatewise medians has the displayed approximation guarantee ([Christodoulou, Sgouritsa, and Vlachos](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.14165)); coordinatewise medians with fixed phantom points are strategyproof ([Agrawal et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.01120)).
For normalized weighted profiles with advice weight \(\lambda\) and lower median ties, the robustness term is already established ([Learning-Augmented Strategyproof Facility Location](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.12829)); no result explicitly establishing the full combined guarantee for this support representation was located.

Modeling choices made when packaging the task

  • Resolved unspecified median ties by choosing the lower weighted median in each coordinate.
  • Discarded zero-mass entries and merged identical locations so that the supplied list is precisely the positive-mass support.
  • Defined common-denominator refinements using denominators shared by resident masses and lambda, with individual mass 1/N and exact advice mass lambda.
  • Specified unilateral individual manipulation, arbitrary real location reports, Euclidean private costs, no payments, and advice and confidence fixed independently of reports.
  • Allowed facility locations throughout the real plane and retained positive optimal cost only as a condition for the approximation ratio.

Prover output (gpt-6.1-sol / high, Bolzano prover prompt)

Result

Yes. The rule is strategyproof on every common-denominator finite refinement, and the full combined approximation guarantee holds.

Write \(a=F_\lambda(\mu,p)\) and \(b=F_0(\mu,p)\), so \(b\) is the coordinatewise lower weighted median without advice. We prove the two advice-dependent bounds directly and apply the established weighted robustness theorem for the third.

An optimal facility \(a^*\) exists: \(C_\mu\) is continuous, and the triangle inequality gives
\[ C_\mu(x)\geq \lVert x\rVert_2-\sum_t\mu_t\lVert z_t\rVert_2, \]
so \(C_\mu\) is coercive. By hypothesis, \(C_\mu(a^*)=\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)>0\).

1. Lower weighted medians minimize absolute-distance cost

Consider finitely many real coordinates \(x_i\) with positive weights \(w_i\), total weight \(T\), and lower weighted median \(q\). Define
\[ W_{<}=\sum_{x_i<q}w_i,\qquad W_{\leq}=\sum_{x_i\leq q}w_i. \]
The definition gives \(W_{<}<T/2\) and \(W_{\leq}\geq T/2\).

Let \(\Phi(s)=\sum_iw_i|x_i-s|\). If \(s>q\), every coordinate at most \(q\) contributes an increase of \(s-q\); every other coordinate contributes a decrease of at most \(s-q\). Therefore
\[ \Phi(s)-\Phi(q)\geq (2W_{\leq}-T)(s-q)\geq0. \]
If \(s<q\), every coordinate at least \(q\) contributes an increase of \(q-s\), while the remaining coordinates contribute a decrease of at most \(q-s\). Hence
\[ \Phi(s)-\Phi(q)\geq (T-2W_{<})(q-s)\geq0. \]
Thus \(q\) minimizes \(\Phi\), including when the median threshold is met exactly.

2. Strategyproofness on every finite refinement

Fix any admissible refinement size \(N\), and put \(K=N\lambda\). Multiplying all masses by \(N\) identifies the rule exactly with the coordinatewise lower median of \(N\) individual reports and \(K\) fixed copies of \(p\). Its order-statistic index in each coordinate is
\[ r=\left\lceil\frac{N+K}{2}\right\rceil. \]
In particular, this identification preserves the specified tie convention.

Fix one individual, arbitrary reports of everyone else, and one coordinate. Let \(x\) be the individual's true coordinate, \(q\) the outcome when she reports \(x\), and \(q'\) the outcome after any deviation. Keep the advice and median threshold fixed.

  • If \(x<q\), the truthful cumulative mass at every \(y<q\) is below the threshold. For \(y<x\), the deviating cumulative mass at \(y\) is at most the truthful cumulative mass at \(x\), which is below the threshold. For \(x\leq y<q\), the truthful report already contributes the individual's full mass, so a deviation cannot increase the cumulative mass there. Consequently \(q'\geq q\), and \(|x-q'|\geq|x-q|\).
  • If \(x>q\), the individual's truthful report contributes no mass at or below \(q\). Thus the other reports and advice alone reach the threshold at \(q\). They remain fixed, so \(q'\leq q\), again giving \(|x-q'|\geq|x-q|\).
  • If \(x=q\), her truthful coordinate distance is zero and cannot decrease.

Apply this argument separately to both coordinates. Each squared coordinate distance weakly increases under a deviation, so their sum and its square root weakly increase. This proves strategyproofness for Euclidean cost, for arbitrary real reports, on every admissible refinement.

3. The bound \(\sqrt{2}\rho\)

For \(j\in\{1,2\}\), define
\[ A_j(s)=\sum_t\mu_t|z_{t,j}-s|. \]
Section 1 applied to the augmented coordinate distribution says that \(a_j\) minimizes
\[ A_j(s)+\lambda|s-p_j|. \]
Comparison with \(s=p_j\) therefore gives
\[ A_j(a_j)+\lambda|a_j-p_j|\leq A_j(p_j), \]
and hence \(A_j(a_j)\leq A_j(p_j)\).

Using \(\lVert v\rVert_2\leq\lVert v\rVert_1\leq\sqrt{2}\lVert v\rVert_2\), we obtain
\[ C_\mu(a) \leq A_1(a_1)+A_2(a_2) \leq A_1(p_1)+A_2(p_2) \leq\sqrt{2}C_\mu(p). \]
Division by the positive optimum proves
\[ \frac{C_\mu(a)}{\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)}\leq\sqrt{2}\rho. \]

4. The bound \(\rho+\sqrt{2}\)

The advised median lies between the ordinary median and the advice

Fix a coordinate \(j\), and let
\[ G_j(s)=\sum_{z_{t,j}\leq s}\mu_t. \]
Since \(b_j\) is the ordinary lower weighted median, \(G_j(s)<1/2\) for \(s<b_j\), while \(G_j(b_j)\geq1/2\).

If \(p_j\geq b_j\), then below \(b_j\) the augmented cumulative mass is \(G_j(s)<1/2\leq(1+\lambda)/2\). At \(p_j\), it is at least
\[ G_j(p_j)+\lambda\geq\frac12+\lambda\geq\frac{1+\lambda}{2}. \]
Thus \(b_j\leq a_j\leq p_j\).

If \(p_j<b_j\), then below \(p_j\) the augmented cumulative mass is again below the threshold. At \(b_j\), it is at least \(1/2+\lambda\), so \(p_j\leq a_j\leq b_j\).

Therefore \(a\) belongs to the closed axis-aligned rectangle with opposite vertices \(p\) and \(b\).

A pointwise rectangle inequality

For any \(z\in\mathbb{R}^2\), the interval property in each coordinate implies
\[ |z_j-a_j|\leq\max\{|z_j-p_j|,|z_j-b_j|\}. \]
Squaring and summing gives
\[ \lVert z-a\rVert_2^2 \leq\lVert z-p\rVert_2^2+\lVert z-b\rVert_2^2 \leq\bigl(\lVert z-p\rVert_2+\lVert z-b\rVert_2\bigr)^2. \]
Taking square roots and summing with weights \(\mu_t\) yields
\[ C_\mu(a)\leq C_\mu(p)+C_\mu(b). \]

Finally, Section 1 shows that each \(b_j\) minimizes \(A_j\). Consequently
\[ C_\mu(b) \leq A_1(b_1)+A_2(b_2) \leq A_1(a^*_1)+A_2(a^*_2) \leq\sqrt{2}C_\mu(a^*). \]
Combining these inequalities proves
\[ \frac{C_\mu(a)}{\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)} \leq\rho+\sqrt{2}. \]

5. The robustness bound and combined guarantee

The Euclidean specialization of Theorem 1 of [Learning-Augmented Strategyproof Facility Location, Section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.12829) establishes the following weighted upper bound: for any finite planar profile with positive weights summing to one, advice weight \(c\in[0,1)\), and coordinatewise lower median selection,
\[ C_\mu(F_c(\mu,p)) \leq \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{1+c}{1-c}\right)^2}\, \operatorname{OPT}(\mu). \]

Its hypotheses hold here: the support is finite, the masses are positive and normalized, the space is two-dimensional Euclidean space, \(c=\lambda<1\), and the median threshold and lower-tie convention agree exactly. Advice may be arbitrary. The factor simplifies to
\[ \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{1+\lambda}{1-\lambda}\right)^2} =\frac{\sqrt{2+2\lambda^2}}{1-\lambda}. \]

All three bounds therefore apply to the same outcome:
\[ \frac{C_\mu(F_\lambda(\mu,p))}{\operatorname{OPT}(\mu)} \leq \min\left\{ \sqrt{2}\rho,\, \rho+\sqrt{2},\, \frac{\sqrt{2\lambda^2+2}}{1-\lambda} \right\}. \]

6. Computing the rule from its support

The refinement is needed only to interpret individual incentives. Computing the facility requires no expansion into individuals.

For each coordinate, sort the \(\tau\) support coordinates together with the advice coordinate, combine equal coordinates, and scan cumulative weights until reaching \((1+\lambda)/2\). This uses \(O(m\tau\log(\tau+1))\) comparisons and arithmetic operations, with \(m=2\).

For a concrete bit bound, take the product \(D\) of the denominators of the masses and \(\lambda\). Its bit length is \(O(L)\). Scale the weights to integers and test the threshold by comparing twice the cumulative integer weight with \(D+D\lambda\). Rational coordinate comparisons and all scaling operations use integers of \(O(L)\) bits. Elementary integer arithmetic thus gives a conservative bound of
\[ O\!\left(m\tau\log(\tau+1)L^2\right) \]
bit operations. The output coordinates are input support coordinates or advice coordinates, so their total encoding length is \(O(L)\).
