Theorem 1 — class: algorithmic

The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given a feasible configuration set \(\mathcal Y\), bounded features \(\phi\), and a finite support \(\mathcal T\) of user types with rational masses \(\mu_r\), let type \(r\) have utility \(u_r(y)=\langle w_r^*,\phi(y)\rangle\). In each round \(q\), choose one shared configuration \(y_q\) maximizing the current estimate of \(U_\mu(y)=\sum_{r\in\mathcal T}\mu_r u_r(y)\), and receive an \(\alpha\)-informative improvement \(\bar y_{rq}\) from a representative of every type, relative to that type’s own best feasible configuration. For type-block estimates updated from each \((y_q,\bar y_{rq})\), does average regret against the best shared configuration admit a bound such as \(\frac{2R\|\Theta^*\|}{\alpha\sqrt Q}+\frac{1}{\alpha Q}\sum_{q=1}^{Q}\sum_{r\in\mathcal T}\mu_r\xi_{rq}\), where \(\Theta^*=(\sqrt{\mu_r}w_r^*)_{r\in\mathcal T}\), or what bound is attainable under these feedback assumptions?

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

A society is given by its support: a list \(\mathcal T\) of \(\tau\geq1\) types with positive binary rational masses \(\mu_r\), normalized by \(\sum_r\mu_r=1\); \(\tau\) is the support size, not the number of possible types.
Let \(\mathcal Y\neq\varnothing\), let \(\phi:\mathcal Y\to\mathbb R^m\) satisfy \(\|\phi(y)\|_2\leq R\), and let each type have a fixed unknown vector \(w_r^*\in\mathbb R^m\), with unrestricted signs, defining \(u_r(y)=\langle w_r^*,\phi(y)\rangle\) and \(U_\mu(y)=\sum_r\mu_r u_r(y)\). Assume all maximizers used below exist and exact maximization is available; ties may be resolved arbitrarily.
Initialize \(w_{r1}=0\). For \(q=1,\ldots,Q\), choose the shared recommendation \(y_q\in\arg\max_{y\in\mathcal Y}\sum_r\mu_r\langle w_{rq},\phi(y)\rangle\), receive one feasible response \(\bar y_{rq}\in\mathcal Y\) from every type, and update \(w_{r,q+1}=w_{rq}+\phi(\bar y_{rq})-\phi(y_q)\).
Feedback satisfies \(u_r(\bar y_{rq})-u_r(y_q)=\alpha(\max_{y\in\mathcal Y}u_r(y)-u_r(y_q))-\xi_{rq}\), where \(\alpha\in(0,1]\) is common to all types and \(\xi_{rq}\in\mathbb R\) may have either sign; responses may depend on the entire history. The learner observes configurations, not utilities or slacks.
Define \(\Theta^*=(\sqrt{\mu_r}w_r^*)_{r\in\mathcal T}\) and \(\operatorname{REG}_Q=Q^{-1}\sum_{q=1}^Q[\max_{y\in\mathcal Y}U_\mu(y)-U_\mu(y_q)]\). Computational costs are measured in \(m,\tau,L,Q\), with \(L\) the binary encoding length of supplied rational data and feature-evaluation and exact-maximization oracle costs charged separately.

Does \(\operatorname{REG}_Q\leq\frac{2R\|\Theta^*\|_2}{\alpha\sqrt Q}+\frac{1}{\alpha Q}\sum_{q=1}^Q\sum_r\mu_r\xi_{rq}\) hold uniformly under these assumptions, or, if it fails, what worst-case regret bound is attainable for this algorithm?

For a single user, the zero-initialized Preference Perceptron has the corresponding regret bound with \(\|w^*\|_2\) and that user's slacks ([Shivaswamy–Joachims](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~tj/publications/shivaswamy_joachims_15a.pdf)).
Multitask coactive learning provides bounds on average improvement effort when each round serves one identified user; it does not analyze simultaneous feedback on a shared social recommendation ([Goetschalckx–Fern–Tadepalli](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/15/Papers/495.pdf)). No result settling the distributional problem specified here was located.

Conclusion

The proposed bound fails, even with zero slacks and exact optimal responses. For every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1]\), a two-type society can have constant positive average regret for arbitrarily many rounds.

A valid uniform replacement is
\[ \operatorname{REG}_Q \leq \min\left\{ 2R\|\Theta^*\|_2,\; \frac{R\|\Theta^*\|_2}{\alpha}\sqrt{1+\frac4Q} +\frac{1}{\alpha Q}\sum_{q,r}\mu_r\xi_{rq} \right\}. \]
Thus, with zero slacks, the worst-case average regret over the allowed societies is \(\Theta(R\|\Theta^*\|_2)\), rather than a quantity tending to zero. Below, a sharper bound identifies the contribution of heterogeneity.

1. A zero-slack counterexample

Let \(m=1\), \(R=1\), \(\mathcal Y=[-1,1]\), and \(\phi(y)=y\). There are two types:
\[ \mu_1=\frac34,\qquad \mu_2=\frac14,\qquad w_1^*=1,\qquad w_2^*=-1. \]
Consequently,
\[ U_\mu(y)=\frac12y,\qquad \max_y U_\mu(y)=\frac12,\qquad \|\Theta^*\|_2=1. \]

For any prescribed \(\alpha\in(0,1]\), give the responses
\[ \bar y_{1q}=(1-\alpha)y_q+\alpha, \qquad \bar y_{2q}=(1-\alpha)y_q-\alpha. \]
Both are feasible: each is a convex combination of \(y_q\) and that type’s optimal configuration. Their improvements satisfy
\[ u_1(\bar y_{1q})-u_1(y_q)=\alpha(1-y_q), \]
and
\[ u_2(\bar y_{2q})-u_2(y_q)=\alpha(1+y_q). \]
These are exactly the required feedback equalities with \(\xi_{rq}=0\).

Define the aggregate learned weight
\[ v_q=\frac34w_{1q}+\frac14w_{2q}. \]
The weighted response is
\[ \frac34\bar y_{1q}+\frac14\bar y_{2q} =(1-\alpha)y_q+\frac{\alpha}{2}, \]
so the aggregate update is
\[ v_{q+1}=v_q+\alpha\left(\frac12-y_q\right). \]

Resolve ties at \(v_q=0\) by choosing \(y_q=1\). Exact maximization then gives the repeating cycle
\[ \begin{array}{c|rrrr} \text{round within cycle}&1&2&3&4\\ \hline v_q/\alpha&0&-\frac12&1&\frac12\\ y_q&1&-1&1&1 \end{array} \]
because the fourth update returns \(v_q\) to zero.

The social regret is zero at \(y_q=1\) and one at \(y_q=-1\). Hence, for every positive integer \(k\),
\[ \operatorname{REG}_{4k}=\frac14. \]
The proposed bound would instead give \(2/(\alpha\sqrt Q)\). It fails whenever \(Q\) is a multiple of four and \(Q>64/\alpha^2\). In particular, at \(\alpha=1\) and \(Q=100\), it asserts \(1/4\leq1/5\).

All masses are positive binary rational numbers, all relevant maxima exist, and both true weights are nonzero.

2. A uniform bound incorporating heterogeneity

Define
\[ \bar w^*=\sum_r\mu_rw_r^*, \qquad \sigma^2=\sum_r\mu_r\|w_r^*-\bar w^*\|_2^2. \]
Expanding the squares and using \(\sum_r\mu_r=1\) yields
\[ \|\Theta^*\|_2^2=\|\bar w^*\|_2^2+\sigma^2. \]

Also define the disagreement between individual and social optimum values:
\[ H=\sum_r\mu_r\max_yu_r(y)-\max_yU_\mu(y). \]
This satisfies \(H\geq0\): evaluate every individual utility at a social maximizer, then bound it by its individual maximum.

Write \(S_Q=\sum_{q=1}^Q\sum_r\mu_r\xi_{rq}\). We will prove
\[ \boxed{ \operatorname{REG}_Q \leq \frac{2R\|\bar w^*\|_2}{\alpha\sqrt Q} +\frac{R\sigma}{\alpha} +\frac{S_Q}{\alpha Q} -H. } \]
No sign restriction on the slacks is needed.

Aggregate-weight control

Let
\[ v_q=\sum_r\mu_rw_{rq}, \qquad x_q=\phi(y_q), \qquad b_q=\sum_r\mu_r\phi(\bar y_{rq}). \]
The algorithm implies \(v_1=0\) and
\[ v_{q+1}=v_q+b_q-x_q. \]

Because \(y_q\) maximizes \(\langle v_q,\phi(y)\rangle\), every feasible response satisfies
\[ \langle v_q,\phi(\bar y_{rq})-x_q\rangle\leq0. \]
Taking the weighted average gives \(\langle v_q,b_q-x_q\rangle\leq0\). Moreover, \(\|b_q\|_2\leq R\), so \(\|b_q-x_q\|_2\leq2R\). Therefore,
\[ \begin{aligned} \|v_{q+1}\|_2^2 &=\|v_q\|_2^2+2\langle v_q,b_q-x_q\rangle +\|b_q-x_q\|_2^2\\ &\leq\|v_q\|_2^2+4R^2. \end{aligned} \]
Induction establishes
\[ \|v_{Q+1}\|_2\leq2R\sqrt Q. \]

Bounding total utility improvement

Put \(\delta_r=w_r^*-\bar w^*\). Since \(\sum_r\mu_r\delta_r=0\), each round’s weighted utility improvement equals
\[ \sum_r\mu_r\bigl[u_r(\bar y_{rq})-u_r(y_q)\bigr] = \langle\bar w^*,b_q-x_q\rangle +\sum_r\mu_r\langle\delta_r,\phi(\bar y_{rq})\rangle. \]
The second term is at most
\[ R\sum_r\mu_r\|\delta_r\|_2 \leq R\sqrt{\sum_r\mu_r\|\delta_r\|_2^2} =R\sigma, \]
where the inequality is weighted Cauchy–Schwarz.

Summing over rounds, telescoping the aggregate updates, and applying Cauchy–Schwarz again gives
\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{q,r}\mu_r\bigl[u_r(\bar y_{rq})-u_r(y_q)\bigr] &\leq\langle\bar w^*,v_{Q+1}\rangle+QR\sigma\\ &\leq2R\|\bar w^*\|_2\sqrt Q+QR\sigma. \end{aligned} \]

On the other hand, the feedback equalities imply exactly
\[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{q,r}\mu_r\bigl[u_r(\bar y_{rq})-u_r(y_q)\bigr] &=\alpha\sum_q \left[\sum_r\mu_r\max_yu_r(y)-U_\mu(y_q)\right]-S_Q\\ &=\alpha Q\bigl(\operatorname{REG}_Q+H\bigr)-S_Q. \end{aligned} \]
Combining these expressions and dividing by \(\alpha Q\) proves the boxed bound.

3. Norm-only bound and worst-case rate

Cauchy–Schwarz and the variance identity give
\[ \frac{2\|\bar w^*\|_2}{\sqrt Q}+\sigma \leq \sqrt{1+\frac4Q}\, \sqrt{\|\bar w^*\|_2^2+\sigma^2} = \sqrt{1+\frac4Q}\,\|\Theta^*\|_2. \]
Dropping the nonpositive term \(-H\) therefore gives
\[ \operatorname{REG}_Q \leq \frac{R\|\Theta^*\|_2}{\alpha}\sqrt{1+\frac4Q} +\frac{S_Q}{\alpha Q}. \]

There is also a utility-range bound independent of feedback. If \(y_\mu^*\) is a social maximizer, then
\[ U_\mu(y_\mu^*)-U_\mu(y_q) = \langle\bar w^*,\phi(y_\mu^*)-\phi(y_q)\rangle \leq2R\|\bar w^*\|_2 \leq2R\|\Theta^*\|_2. \]
Taking the minimum proves the uniform replacement stated at the beginning.

To verify the matching worst-case order, scale the counterexample by setting \(\phi(y)=Ry\) and \(w_1^*=B,\ w_2^*=-B\), for arbitrary \(R,B>0\). Its recommendations follow the same cycle, while
\[ \|\Theta^*\|_2=B, \qquad \operatorname{REG}_{4k}=\frac{RB}{4}. \]
Together with the upper bound \(2RB\), this establishes constant-order worst-case average regret, even with zero slacks, for every permitted \(\alpha\).

When all true vectors coincide, \(\sigma=H=0\), and the sharper bound recovers the proposed decaying bound.

4. Why the single-user proof does not transfer

The [single-user Preference Perceptron proof, Theorem 1](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~tj/publications/shivaswamy_joachims_15a.pdf), uses the fact that the learned weight has nonpositive inner product with its update.

Here, shared maximization controls the aggregate inner product \(\langle v_q,b_q-x_q\rangle\). It does not imply
\[ \sum_r\mu_r \langle w_{rq},\phi(\bar y_{rq})-\phi(y_q)\rangle \leq0. \]
Thus the individual weights’ stacked norm need not grow only as \(\sqrt Q\). The heterogeneity term in the proved bound accounts for precisely this missing control.

5. Computational accounting

The recommendations can be implemented by maintaining only \(v_q\), since its update exactly reproduces the aggregate of the stated individual updates. Each round uses one exact-maximization call, feature evaluations for the recommendation and the \(\tau\) responses, and \(O(m\tau)\) arithmetic operations. Total arithmetic work is \(O(Qm\tau)\), with no dependence on the number of possible types or on inverse masses.

For rational feature outputs, bit costs are polynomial in their encoding lengths, the supplied rational-data length, and \(Q\), with oracle costs charged separately. For arbitrary real feature outputs, the stated accounting is an arithmetic-operation count; the premises do not specify a finite encoding for those outputs.
