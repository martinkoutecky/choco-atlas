Result

Every truth-recovering stream satisfies the guarantee. In fact, \(\hat q_t=q_t^*\) at every time, so the tracking error is identically zero. This follows directly from the specified posterior and update rule.

Proof

Fix any time \(t\) and cohort \(r\). Since \(0\leq p_{r,s,\hat y_s}\leq1\), the cumulative count satisfies

\(0\leq C_{r,t}=\mu_r\sum_{s=1}^{t}p_{r,s,\hat y_s}\leq t\mu_r.\)

Define

\(A_{r,t}=\lambda C_{r,t}+\alpha-1,\qquad B_{r,t}=\lambda(t\mu_r-C_{r,t})+\beta-1.\)

The hypotheses \(\alpha,\beta>1\) and \(\lambda>0\) imply \(A_{r,t}>0\) and \(B_{r,t}>0\), including when the cumulative count is at either endpoint.

The posterior kernel is positive on \((0,1)^\tau\) and bounded above by \(1\), so its integral is finite and strictly positive. Thus the normalized posterior exists, and its logarithm has the form

\(\log f_t(q)=c_t+\sum_{r=1}^{\tau}\bigl[A_{r,t}\log q_r+B_{r,t}\log(1-q_r)\bigr],\)

where \(c_t\) is independent of \(q\).

For each coordinate, let \(g_{r,t}(u)=A_{r,t}\log u+B_{r,t}\log(1-u)\) on \((0,1)\). Its derivatives are

\(g'_{r,t}(u)=\dfrac{A_{r,t}}{u}-\dfrac{B_{r,t}}{1-u},\qquad g''_{r,t}(u)=-\dfrac{A_{r,t}}{u^2}-\dfrac{B_{r,t}}{(1-u)^2}<0.\)

Consequently, \(g_{r,t}\) is strictly concave. Its unique stationary point belongs to \((0,1)\) and satisfies

\(A_{r,t}(1-u)=B_{r,t}u,\qquad u=\dfrac{A_{r,t}}{A_{r,t}+B_{r,t}}.\)

Strict concavity makes this point its unique global maximum. Since the coordinates are unconstrained apart from belonging to \((0,1)\), their individual maxima give the unique maximum of the full log posterior. Therefore,

\((q_t^*)_r=\dfrac{A_{r,t}}{A_{r,t}+B_{r,t}}=\dfrac{\lambda C_{r,t}+\alpha-1}{\lambda t\mu_r+\alpha+\beta-2}=\hat q_{r,t}.\)

This holds for every cohort, proving \(\hat q_t=q_t^*\). Hence

\(\|\hat q_t-q_t^*\|_\infty=0,\qquad \lim_{t\to\infty}\sqrt t\,\|\hat q_t-q_t^*\|_\infty=0,\)

which is precisely the requested \(o(t^{-1/2})\) guarantee.

Scope and the maximum/minimum distinction

The proof does not require truth recovery: both quantities use the same selected-label counts, so the identity holds for every admissible stream. Truth recovery additionally identifies those counts with the true-label counts.

The calculation is the fractional-count counterpart of the posterior and MAP update in equations (6)–(7) of the [original source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.00007). No asymptotic convergence theorem is needed here.

The choice of maximum is essential. For the specified posterior, sending any coordinate to \(0\) while fixing the others makes \(\log f_t(q)\) tend to \(-\infty\), because \(A_{r,t}>0\). Thus the log posterior has no minimum on \((0,1)^\tau\). The task’s explicitly defined posterior maximum, however, exists uniquely and equals the update exactly.
