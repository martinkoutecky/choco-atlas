The continuous mirror question, as graded

For each population size \(N\), let a finite-support measure \(\mu\) on a Ptolemaic metric space have masses in multiples of \(1/N\) and support size at most \(\tau\). Among lottery rules mapping \(\mu\) to pairs of facility locations and remaining strategyproof when one individual moves mass \(1/N\) from their true location to any reported location, what is the infimum worst-case ratio of expected per-capita social cost to \(\mathrm{OPT}(\mu)\) as \(N\) grows with \(\tau\) fixed?

Packaged proof task (what the prover saw — nothing else)

Let \((\Omega,d)\) range over Ptolemaic metric spaces: \(d(a,c)d(b,e)\le d(a,b)d(c,e)+d(a,e)d(b,c)\) for all four points.
A society is a probability measure \(\mu=\sum_{i=1}^{\tau}p_i\delta_{x_i}\), supplied as its distinct support locations and positive binary rational masses. Here \(\tau\) is the actual support size, not the number of possible types; require \(Np_i\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\). Fix a support bound \(t\ge3\), and evaluate performance only when \(\tau\le t\).
There are \(m=2\) facilities, allowed anywhere in \(\Omega\), with coincident locations permitted and no transfers. Write \(c(x,\ell)=\min\{d(x,\ell_1),d(x,\ell_2)\}\), \(C(F_N,\mu)=\mathbb E_{\ell\sim F_N(\mu)}\int c(x,\ell)\,d\mu(x)\), and \(\mathrm{OPT}(\mu)=\inf_{\ell\in\Omega^2}\int c(x,\ell)\,d\mu(x)\).
A lottery rule \(F_N\) is defined on every such society, without a support cap, and must satisfy \(\mathbb E_{F_N(\mu)}c(x,\ell)\le\mathbb E_{F_N(\mu-N^{-1}\delta_x+N^{-1}\delta_y)}c(x,\ell)\) whenever \(\mu(\{x\})\ge N^{-1}\) and \(y\in\Omega\). Thus deviations may introduce a new support location.
Define \(r(F_N,\mu)=C(F_N,\mu)/\mathrm{OPT}(\mu)\) for positive optimum; for zero optimum set it to \(1\) if the expected cost is zero and \(+\infty\) otherwise. Let \(\mathcal F_N\) range over families of these rules, one for each Ptolemaic space, and set \(R(t)=\limsup_{N\to\infty}\inf_{\mathcal F_N}\sup_{(\Omega,d),\,\mu:\,\tau\le t}r(F_N,\mu)\).
No computational restriction is imposed on the lotteries. State any running times in \(m=2\), \(\tau\), the input bit length \(L\), \(N\), \(t\), and the metric representation or distance-oracle costs.

What is the optimal asymptotic worst-case approximation ratio \(R(t)\) for each fixed support bound \(t\ge3\)?

For finite agents, a strategyproof mixture achieves tight ratio \(11/3\) on Ptolemaic spaces, and every randomized strategyproof mechanism has a worst-case lower bound \((1+\sqrt2)/2\), already on the line ([Ma–Peng](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09061v1)).
