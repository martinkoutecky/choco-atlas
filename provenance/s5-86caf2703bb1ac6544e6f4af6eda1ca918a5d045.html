The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given a connected network type graph \(H=(T\cup\{s\},E)\), nonnegative rational edge costs \(w\), positive rational masses \(\mu_t\) with \(\sum_{t\in T}\mu_t=1\), and nonnegative per-unit allocations \(a_t\), with \(\sum_{t\in T}\mu_t a_t=\operatorname{MST}_H(T\cup\{s\})\), does there exist a mass vector \(y\) with \(0\le y_t\le\mu_t\) such that, for \(A=\{t:y_t>0\}\), \(\sum_{t\in T}a_t y_t>\operatorname{MST}_{H[A\cup\{s\}]}(A\cup\{s\})\)?
