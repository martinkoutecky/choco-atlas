The continuous mirror question, as graded

Conclusion

The requested exact equilibrium computation is polynomial-time solvable. A change of units reduces the given market to the market treated by Garg–Végh. The reduction preserves the utility graph and permits exact recovery of the required prices and aggregate allocations.

Reduction to unit supplies

Write each mass as \(\mu_s=a_s/b_s\), where \(a_s,b_s\) are positive integers. Set

\(D=\prod_{s\in T}b_s\), and \(v_{ts}=D\mu_su_{ts}\).

Every \(v_{ts}\) is a nonnegative integer. Construct an auxiliary market with one agent and one good for each element of \(T\). Agent \(t\) owns one unit of good \(t\), and its utility coefficient for good \(s\) is \(v_{ts}\).

This construction rescales quantities: one auxiliary unit of good \(s\) represents \(\mu_s\) original units. The common factor \(D\) does not affect utility maximization.

Because \(D\mu_s>0\), we have \(v_{ts}>0\) if and only if \(u_{ts}>0\). Thus the auxiliary market has exactly the original directed utility graph. Every agent likes some good, every good is liked, and every singleton strongly connected component has a self-loop.

Applicable exact algorithm

Garg–Végh’s Lemma 2.3 states that, under the preceding assumptions on agents and goods, a positive-price equilibrium exists precisely when every singleton strongly connected component has a self-loop. Their preliminaries explain the reduction from such markets to strongly connected markets, and Theorem 3.1 provides a strongly polynomial algorithm for computing an exact equilibrium. Their model also explicitly accommodates arbitrary initial endowments through reduction. Consequently, the auxiliary market satisfies the required hypotheses. [Garg–Végh, Sections 2–3](https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/113604/3/AD_final.pdf).

Run this algorithm and obtain rational prices \(q_s>0\) and nonnegative money flows \(f_{ts}\). By the definition of equilibrium in their model, these satisfy

\(\sum_t f_{ts}=q_s\), and \(\sum_s f_{ts}=q_t\),

and whenever \(f_{ts}>0\),

\(\displaystyle \frac{v_{ts}}{q_s}=\max_{r\in T}\frac{v_{tr}}{q_r}\).

Recovering the requested equilibrium

Define the positive rational constant \(c=\mu_{t_0}/q_{t_0}\), and set

\(\displaystyle p_s=\frac{c q_s}{\mu_s}\), and \(\displaystyle X_{ts}=\frac{\mu_s f_{ts}}{q_s}\).

All denominators are positive, so these are well-defined rational numbers, with \(p_s>0\) and \(X_{ts}\geq0\). We verify each required condition.

Normalization. By the definition of \(c\),

\(\displaystyle p_{t_0}=\frac{c q_{t_0}}{\mu_{t_0}}=1\).

Market clearing. For every good \(s\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_tX_{ts}=\frac{\mu_s}{q_s}\sum_tf_{ts}=\mu_s\).

Budget exhaustion. For every type \(t\),

\(\displaystyle \sum_sp_sX_{ts}=\sum_s\frac{cq_s}{\mu_s}\frac{\mu_sf_{ts}}{q_s}=c\sum_sf_{ts}=cq_t=\mu_tp_t\).

Utility maximization. For every pair \(t,s\),

\(\displaystyle \frac{v_{ts}}{q_s}=\frac{D\mu_su_{ts}}{q_s}=Dc\,\frac{u_{ts}}{p_s}\).

The factor \(Dc>0\) is independent of \(s\), so the maximizing goods coincide in the two markets. Moreover, \(X_{ts}>0\) implies \(f_{ts}>0\). The auxiliary equilibrium condition therefore gives exactly

\(\displaystyle X_{ts}>0\ \Longrightarrow\ \frac{u_{ts}}{p_s}=\max_{r\in T}\frac{u_{tr}}{p_r}\).

No choice among tied maximizers is prescribed or needed.

Binary running time and exactness

The binary length of \(D\) is at most one plus the sum of the binary lengths of the denominators \(b_s\), hence is \(O(L)\). Each transformed coefficient \(v_{ts}\) also has \(O(L)\) bits. The auxiliary input therefore has length polynomial in the original input length and support size.

Garg–Végh use the standard definition of strong polynomiality: polynomially many arithmetic operations, with all intermediate rational numbers having encoding length polynomial in the input length. Thus their exact algorithm has polynomial binary running time on this auxiliary input. [Garg–Végh, Section 1](https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/113604/3/AD_final.pdf).

The subsequent formulas use polynomially many rational arithmetic operations and preserve polynomial encoding length. They therefore produce the requested exact rational output in time polynomial in \(m,\tau,L\). Here the algorithm has only \(\tau=m\) agents and goods; no enumeration of absent types occurs.
