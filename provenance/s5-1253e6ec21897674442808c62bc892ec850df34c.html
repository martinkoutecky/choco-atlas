$$ P_A(z)\ge\alpha^{1+b(z)}P_i(z), \qquad Q_A(z)\ge\alpha^2Q_i(z) $$

$$ p_A\ge\alpha p_i. $$

$$ \beta_A\ge\alpha^2\beta_i. $$
Consequently,
$$ p_A^2\ge\alpha^2p_i^2. $$

We check that one tick preserves the inequalities.

Its transition probabilities depend on the sampled real-counter marginal, independently of the tagged agent’s opinion and intermediate opinion. Seeding. A newly sampled intermediate opinion has colored source masses
