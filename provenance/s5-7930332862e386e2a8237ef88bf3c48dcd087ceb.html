The continuous mirror question, as graded

Given \(m\) item categories, type masses \(\mu_A=\mu_B=1/2\), per-capita supplies \(q_g=1/2\), additive type valuations \(v^A,v^B\) with common total \(T\), and \(\alpha\le T/3\), construct or rule out a deterministic rule \(\mathcal M_\infty\) mapping every reported pair to a partition \((S_A,S_B)\) such that each type receives its integral bundle, the outcome is PMMS and Pareto optimal, ordinally equivalent reports preserve true utility, and every permitted perturbation \(v^{i'}\) satisfies \(1/(2+12\alpha/T)\le v^i(\mathcal M_\infty(v^{i'},v^{-i})_i)/v^i(\mathcal M_\infty(v^i,v^{-i})_i)\le 2+12\alpha/T\).

Existence and construction.

A deterministic rule \(\mathcal M_\infty\) satisfying all the requirements exists. Take rank-leximin with a fixed, report-independent tie-breaking order.

For each reported valuation \(v^i\), define the rank of a bundle \(S\subseteq G\) by
\[ r_i(S)=\bigl|\{R\subseteq G:v^i(R)\leq v^i(S)\}\bigr|. \]
Here and below, this formula is read as \(r_i(S)=|\{R\subseteq G:v^i(R)\leq v^i(S)\}|\).

For each feasible partition \((S_A,S_B)\), form the pair
\(\bigl(\min\{r_A(S_A),r_B(S_B)\},\max\{r_A(S_A),r_B(S_B)\}\bigr)\).
Choose a partition whose pair is lexicographically maximal, breaking remaining ties by the fixed order. This defines \(\mathcal M_\infty\) at every reported profile.

The theorem being applied.

Menon–Larson’s Theorems 3 and 5 establish that rank-leximin for two agents returns a PMMS and Pareto-optimal partition and is \((2+12\alpha/T,\alpha)\)-approximately stable for every \(0<\alpha\leq T/3\). Their Definition 10 includes exact utility invariance under ordinally equivalent reports. Their framework permits bundle-value ties, and the paper explicitly extends its results to real-valued valuations. [Menon–Larson, Sections 2 and 4.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.15203).

Verification of the hypotheses and transfer.

There are exactly two supported, labeled types. Associate them with the two agents in the theorem and associate each category with one indivisible good. The task specifies that feasible outcomes are precisely ordered partitions of \(G\), so this association preserves the entire outcome domain.

Every admissible report is additive, strictly positive on singletons, and has the common total \(T\). Binary-rational reports are therefore within the theorem’s real-valued domain.

For either type \(i\), the two allocated bundles together equal \(G\). Consequently, the theorem’s pairwise maximin-share threshold is exactly
\(\max_{R\subseteq G}\min\{v^i(R),v^i(G\setminus R)\}\),
as required here. Pareto dominance also compares exactly the same feasible partitions and the same two utilities. Thus both fairness and efficiency transfer directly.

Ordinal equivalence in the task preserves every subset comparison, including ties. It therefore preserves every rank \(r_i(S)\). Holding the other report fixed leaves all partition scores unchanged; fixed tie-breaking then returns the same partition. This proves the requested utility equality.

A permitted perturbation has the same total, preserves the singleton weak order, and has singleton \(\ell_1\) distance at most \(\alpha\). These are precisely the theorem’s neighboring-report conditions. Its stability conclusion therefore gives
\(\frac{1}{2+12\alpha/T}\leq \frac{v^i(\mathcal M_\infty(v^{i'},v^{-i})_i)} {v^i(\mathcal M_\infty(v^i,v^{-i})_i)} \leq 2+12\alpha/T\).

The denominator is positive: because \(m\geq2\) and singleton values are positive, splitting off any singleton gives two positive-valued bundles. Hence the PMMS threshold is positive.

Running time.

An exact implementation enumerates all \(2^m\) subsets and computes their values for each of the \(\tau=2\) types. Sort each type’s subset values; every tied block receives its final position as its rank. Then scan the \(2^m\) ordered partitions and select the maximum score.

Clearing the input denominators produces integers of \(O(L)\) bits. Subset evaluation and sorting require \(O(\tau m2^m)\) arithmetic operations and comparisons. Using elementary exact arithmetic gives the conservative bit-operation bound \(O(\tau m2^mL^2)\), with \(\tau=2\). Exponential running time poses no obstacle to the stated existence requirement.
