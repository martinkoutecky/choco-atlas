$$ f(\mathbf u)\le0 $$

$$ \begin{pmatrix} a(\mathbf u)&b(\mathbf u)\\ b(\mathbf u)&c(\mathbf u) \end{pmatrix}, $$

$$ \sqrt{(a(\mathbf u)-c(\mathbf u))^2+4b(\mathbf u)^2} -a(\mathbf u)-c(\mathbf u)\le0. $$

$$ \frac{a+c\pm\sqrt{(a-c)^2+4b^2}}{2}, $$

so the inequality is exactly nonnegativity of the smaller eigenvalue.

$$ \mathbf u\in[-1,1]^d,\qquad f_1(\mathbf u)\le0,\ldots,f_s(\mathbf u)\le0, $$

where each $f_k$ is affine or has the form

$$ f_k(\mathbf u)=\sqrt{S_k(\mathbf u)}-T_k(\mathbf u). $$

Here $S_k$ is the sum of squares of two rational affine expressions, and $T_k$ is rational affine. Every $f_k$ is finite, continuous, and convex globally: the square root is a Euclidean norm of an affine map.

$$ C(\mathbf q+a\mathbf e)=C(\mathbf q)+a $$

for all $\mathbf q$ and all real $a$.
